===================================================================
FARMOOLOGY - HOW TO ADD OFFERS & NOTIFICATIONS
===================================================================

You have two very easy ways to show an offer or notification on your website:

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1 (EASIEST - ZERO CODE):
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Save your offer, discount, or announcement photo in this folder.
2. Name it:
      offer1.jpg   (or offer1.png)
   or offer2.jpg
   or notification.jpg
3. That's it! When anyone visits your website, an attractive popup
   notification will automatically appear showing your offer photo
   with a direct WhatsApp enquiry button.

To remove an offer:
- Just delete or rename the image file in this folder.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2 (CUSTOM TITLE & LINK):
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Open the file 'offers.json' in this folder:
- Set "enabled": true (or false to hide notifications)
- Put your image name (e.g. "offers/offer1.jpg")
- Customize the title, subtitle, or button text
- Add your WhatsApp message link

When you are ready to publish online:
- Push the changes to GitHub, and it will be live immediately!
