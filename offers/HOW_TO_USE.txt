FARMOOLOGY - FESTIVAL & SPECIAL OFFERS POPUP GUIDE
===================================================================

Just like top e-commerce websites (Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy):
Whenever a festival, seasonal sale, or new batch training comes:

-------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO DISPLAY YOUR FESTIVAL / OFFER FLYER:
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Drop your flyer or poster picture into this 'offers' folder.
2. You can name it ANY of these:
   - offer.jpg      (or offer.png)
   - festival.jpg   (or festival.png)
   - flyer.jpg      (or flyer.png)
   - banner.jpg     (or banner.png)
   - offer1.jpg     (or offer1.png)

3. THAT IS ALL!
   - Your full flyer/poster will appear as a stunning festival popup
     when visitors open the website!
   - Clicking directly on the flyer poster opens WhatsApp to chat
     and claim the offer.
   - A floating [🎁 Special Offer] bell button also appears on the
     bottom-left of the site so visitors can view it anytime!

-------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO REMOVE AN OFFER:
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Simply delete the image file from this folder, and the popup
will disappear completely from the website.
